GE Aerospace Form 10-K, fiscal year 2024. NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS.
Note -- Discontinued Operations: On January 3, 2023, we completed the previously announced separation of our HealthCare business, structured as a tax-free spin-off, through GE's pro-rata distribution of approximately 80.1% of the outstanding shares of GE HealthCare. On April 2, 2024, we completed the previously announced separation of GE Vernova, our energy business, on similar terms. GE's continuing operations following the GE Vernova separation, and following the earlier GE HealthCare separation, are referred to as GE Aerospace. Results of both GE HealthCare and GE Vernova are reported as discontinued operations for all periods presented in these consolidated financial statements.
