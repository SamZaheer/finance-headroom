IBM Corporation Form 10-K, fiscal year 2022. NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS.
Note -- Divestitures: On November 3, 2021, the company completed the separation of its managed infrastructure services unit into a new public company, Kyndryl Holdings, Inc., with the distribution of 80.1 percent of the outstanding shares of Kyndryl to IBM stockholders on a pro rata basis. The accounting requirements for reporting the separation of Kyndryl as a discontinued operation were met when the separation was completed. Accordingly, the historical results of Kyndryl have been presented as discontinued operations in these consolidated financial statements.
