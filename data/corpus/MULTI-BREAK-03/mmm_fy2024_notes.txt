3M Company Form 10-K, fiscal year 2024. NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS.
Note -- Separation of Solventum: On April 1, 2024, 3M completed the previously announced separation of its Health Care business through a pro rata distribution of 80.1% of the outstanding shares of Solventum Corporation to 3M shareholders, structured as a tax-free spin-off. The historical results of Solventum are reported in 3M's consolidated financial statements as discontinued operations for all periods presented.
